## Candidate contract

The public 8/10/12/15 percent ladder remains visible and the wallet remains
available. A top-up creates only refundable principal. Benefit is earned from
the amount actually used and not returned, independently per principal lot.
The cumulative target is the better of ordinary cashback and the deposit
entitlement, never their sum. Ordinary value is immediate; only the positive
deposit uplift is held until acceptance plus 14 days. Subscriptions are
excluded. A qualifying first referral settles exactly 200 points once.

Partial order refunds use cumulative totals and an explicit deposit-funded
component. A 60,000 RUB deposit-funded order returned by 30,000 RUB leaves
30,000 RUB NET principal and exactly 3,000 points of entitlement. Replaying
the same cumulative refund is a no-op; completing the refund creates revision
2 and leaves zero entitlement. Mixed-source partial refunds fail closed unless
their exact composition is supplied.

Order-level refund tombstones are durable and `complete` is true if and only if
the cumulative monetary refund equals the paid total. They are checked under
the same `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` lock as reward issuance. Spent-point restoration
tracks a separate cumulative bonus component: a partial return restores only
the explicit delta, while a complete return restores the exact remainder, each
with an order/audit CAS and one ledger entry in the same transaction.

A cancellation atomically releases `reserved|confirming` internal deposit
payments. If settlement wins the race first, the same refund transaction moves
only the returned NET principal from `principal_consumed` back to
`principal_available`, updates a cumulative `pay_return` ledger, cancels the
internal payment and releases its operation. Both schedules therefore finish
as `canceled + released + principal returned + durable tombstone`.
