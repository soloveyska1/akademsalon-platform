## Live economic contract

The existing campaign ID, `ПЕРВЫЙЛИСТ` code, family, end date and durable
suppression keys were preserved. Welcome is now 12% of one first order from
2,500 RUB, capped at 5,000 RUB. The visible scale is 300 RUB at 2,500, 600 RUB
at 5,000, 1,200 RUB at 10,000, 2,400 RUB at 20,000 and at most 5,000 RUB from
about 42,000 RUB. Qualified return-to-draft is 10% from 5,000 RUB, capped at
2,500 RUB and valid for 72 hours.

The server keeps promo and subscription best-of rather than stacking them.
Spent bonus points plus the winning promo/subscription benefit cannot exceed
25% of the agreed price. Standalone promo exposure is therefore bounded at
12%/10%, and at least 75% remains before fulfilment cost under every supported
benefit combination. This is bounded acquisition spend, not proof that the
discount is costless: positive contribution margin still requires
authoritative fulfilment-cost and commission data.
