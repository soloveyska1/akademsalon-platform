## Defect and bounded result

All nine discipline landings showed one entry orientation but published hidden
cheaper offers in Service JSON-LD. The strongest example was the psychology
VKR page: the visible orientation was `от 29 000 ₽`, while its
`AggregateOffer.lowPrice` was `5 000` and two additional offers were absent
from the rendered page. This was a deterministic content mismatch, not a
conversion inference.

Each Service now has exactly one `Offer` with one
`PriceSpecification.minPrice`, equal to the visible orientation and expressed
in RUB. The nine values are 24,000 / 29,000 / 24,000 for VKR and 9,000 /
11,500 / 9,000 / 9,000 / 11,000 / 9,000 for course pages. The old
`AggregateOffer`, `lowPrice`, `offerCount`, nested offers and invisible 5,000
orientation are absent.

This follows the Schema.org relationship
`Offer.priceSpecification -> PriceSpecification.minPrice` and Google's general
rule that structured data must represent visible page content. It does not
claim a rich result, higher ranking, CTR or conversion uplift; `Service` has no
guaranteed dedicated Google rich-result treatment.
