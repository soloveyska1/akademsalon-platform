## Reproduced defect

On the owner-visible ordinary three-step configurator at 1128×804 the fixed
launcher occupied `y=306..348`, while the third step occupied
`y=266.6..318.6`. The rectangles intersected. The root cause was the shared
`top:306px` rule combined with appending the control directly to `body`; that
coordinate happened not to collide with the shorter two-step VIP rail.

## Bounded repair

At desktop widths the existing owner-only launcher now participates in the
sidebar flex flow immediately before `.tx-wizard-help`. At widths up to 920px
the same node moves to `body` as a compact fixed control above the mobile task
shelf and safe-area inset. A `matchMedia` listener reuses one node across live
resizes. Focus intent is preserved during that reparenting and restored with
`preventScroll` when the browser temporarily moves focus to `body`.

The explicit-click-only, non-redeemable and GET-only owner-preview contract is
unchanged. Prices, deposits, schedules, eligibility, discount calculations,
best-of selection, redemption and order submission were not modified.
