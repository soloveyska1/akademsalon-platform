## Outcome and economic boundary

The existing `ПЕРВЫЙЛИСТ` identity, family, dates and durable suppression keys
remain unchanged. The welcome offer is now 12% of the agreed first-order price
from 2,500 RUB, capped at 5,000 RUB. Exact displayed examples are:

- 2,500 RUB -> 300 RUB;
- 5,000 RUB -> 600 RUB;
- 10,000 RUB -> 1,200 RUB;
- 20,000 RUB -> 2,400 RUB;
- about 42,000 RUB and above -> at most 5,000 RUB.

The qualified return-to-draft offer is 10% from 5,000 RUB, capped at 2,500 RUB
and valid for 72 hours. It remains smaller than welcome at every common price,
is issued only after the existing final-stage/60-visible-second explicit-exit
gate and is suppressed when welcome is already applied. Neither offer stacks
with another promo or Salon+; the server selects the larger discount.

The standalone campaign exposure is bounded by 12%/10%, and promo or
subscription plus spent bonus points is bounded by 25% of the agreed price.
This is a controlled acquisition/retention expense, not a claim that the
discount is literally costless. Positive contribution margin cannot be proved
without authoritative fulfilment cost and commission data. Before those costs,
at least 75% of the agreed price remains after all currently supported benefits.

## Eligibility, presentation and owner visibility

Old accounts, prior orders/paid relationships, known guest sessions, historical
contacts and prior family claims remain ineligible and receive neither asset nor
dialog. A clean anonymous browser still receives only provisional presentation;
the order transaction is the authoritative contact/account/claim gate. Existing
v1 seen/dismissed keys continue to suppress people who already saw the sheet.

Salon uses its authenticated `owner_preview` state; Kladovaya keeps the
non-persistent `?offer_preview=welcome` visual preview. Both are explicitly
labelled as preview, issue no code and grant no entitlement. Kladovaya retains
its own visual language, states that Academic Salon is a separate paid site and
uses one outbound CTA. Salon retains its manuscript/wax visual language. Both
surfaces state 12%, 5,000 RUB, one first order and the expiry in plain language.
Retention distinguishes applying 10% and continuing from saving the draft and
leaving; no fake countdown or guaranteed saving claim was added.

## Authoritative backend and repricing safety

The v2 upgrade pins and moves one coherent six-source set: webapp, database,
promo, bonus, order-choice and subscription services. It migrates welcome,
already-issued retention rows and future retention issuance without changing
claims, tokens, order prices, stored discounts or campaign identity. A unique
database sentinel, private snapshot and exact PID/start-time/cwd/cgroup
attestation split staging from restart/finalize; mixed source states fail closed.

Promo, subscription best-of, finite-use CAS, bonus consumption/refund, order
update and events now share `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transactions. Regression covers:

- concurrent promo/subscription/bonus writers and two orders competing for one
  remaining generic promo use;
- a 30,000 RUB order with 3,600 RUB promo and 3,900 spent points repriced to
  2,000 RUB: stale promo is removed, 3,500 points are returned and only 400
  points remain, below both the 20% points and 25% aggregate ceilings;
- no-active-subscription and invalid/minimum promo public paths, not only the
  database helper;
- a finite claimed retention code applied at 10,000 RUB, cleared below its
  5,000 RUB minimum and restored at 10,000 RUB without a second decrement;
  another order remains `promo_used_up`;
- an exact dormant promo that previously lost to Salon+ is restored when the
  subscription disappears or becomes weaker; a first finite win performs CAS
  1 -> 0, while an unclaimed dormant code is never activated;
- a claim accepted before the deployment sentinel keeps its promised rate
  through temporary inactive/expired/used-up states only when
  `family + code + order_id` match exactly.

Review also found that the installed v1 `promo.py` had lost `why_invalid` during
its earlier source rewrite: syntax compilation passed, but runtime code checking
could fail. The v2 aggregate patch restores the complete validity function and
fails on a duplicate or unexpected anchor. The executable public-path test calls
the real restored function for minimum and inactive states without a stub.

Safe rollback keeps the coherent v2 source set and the aggregate guard, turns
new presentation/issuance/claims off and preserves already-promised rows. The
release runbook forbids a separate kill-switch toggle between economics staging
and finalize/rollback: replacing the transition sentinel in that short window
would require manual restoration of the snapshotted active flags before a later
enable. No such toggle is part of the release sequence.
