## Defect and bounded result

The prospective production sample after REL-0175 contained real route changes
and contact-step reach but no `first_input`. Readback of the shipped DOM and
listener showed the cause: the one-shot listener was attached to hidden legacy
`#confBody`, while the current dynamic wizard is its sibling under `#main`.

REL-0178 delegates the same consent-gated listener from stable `#main`. It still
fires once, sends only the allowlisted CTA and never sends a field name, value
or other form content. Contract 2.4.1 clarifies the label without changing the
strict funnel event set or ordering.

The backend also exposes aggregate diagnostic counts for sessions that opened
the configurator, meaningfully continued and reached the correct route-specific
contact step. A nearest-prior `config_open` in full event order separates
calculator and service routes, including mixed same-session openings. These
metrics are explicitly diagnostic and do not redefine strict conversion.
