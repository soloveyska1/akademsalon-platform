## Outcome and bounded economics

`ПЕРВЫЙЛИСТ` is one first-order-only welcome offer shared by the Salon and the
Kladovaya referral surface. It grants 2% of the agreed order price from 2,500
RUB and is capped at 2,500 RUB. Reviewed examples are exact:

- 2,500 RUB -> 50 RUB;
- 20,000 RUB -> 400 RUB;
- 60,000 RUB -> 1,200 RUB;
- 125,000 RUB and above -> at most 2,500 RUB.

The return-to-draft offer is deliberately smaller: 1% from 5,000 RUB, capped
at 1,000 RUB and valid for 72 hours. Both discounts are monotonic, mutually
exclusive and go through the existing best-of discount calculation; neither
stacks with another promo. The existing aggregate benefit ceiling remains
unchanged. A database setting is the immediate campaign kill switch.

The campaign is scheduled through 21 September 2026. Public terms state the
formula, cap, first-order condition, non-stacking rule and final server
authority. No revenue or conversion uplift is claimed before live measurement.

## Eligibility, atomic claim and abuse boundary

Presentation and redemption are separate gates. The Salon first calls a
private/no-store eligibility endpoint. Existing accounts, accounts with an
order or a prior paid relationship, known guest-order sessions, claimed identities, admin
preview sessions, disabled campaigns and expired campaigns cannot receive a
redeemable state.

A truly clean anonymous browser cannot be linked to historical identity before
the visitor supplies a contact. That presentation is labelled provisional in
the public terms. The order transaction then canonicalises email aliases,
Russian/international phone forms and Telegram/VK handles and URLs, scans prior
guest contacts, claim contacts and every other account's normalised contact,
validates campaign state again, inserts the order and stores the unique family
claim atomically. A newly registered account therefore cannot erase guest or
cross-account history. Concurrent duplicate claims cannot both commit.

Retention additionally requires:

- a Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax intent cookie issued on the configurator;
- an intent at least 60 seconds old and no more than 72 hours old;
- the final contact stage, at least 60 visible active seconds and a quote band
  of 5,000 RUB or more;
- no known old account/guest session and no prior family claim;
- one token-bound, one-use random code, with at most 40 grants globally per UTC
  day.

New retention grants stop at 18 September 23:59:59 Moscow time
(20:59:59 UTC), exactly 72 hours before the campaign ends at 21 September
23:59:59 Moscow time, so every issued code can receive the full advertised
term. A normal 300-point Telegram welcome accrual does not reclassify a
post-launch first-order account as an old customer; the existing 25% aggregate
benefit ceiling still applies.

Grant validation and consumption occur in the same order transaction. Turning
the campaign setting off blocks eligibility, code check, grant issuance and
new order claims. Rollback restores only the three pinned source files and
turns the campaign off; it never replaces the live SQLite database, so orders
created after the backup survive.

## Privacy and cross-site boundary

Kladovaya sends only allowlisted categorical route/attribution values and the
public promo code to `https://akademsalon.ru/configurator.html`. Before showing
the sheet it calls its own `/promo-status`; that server route checks the Salon
kill switch without forwarding the visitor request, cookies, headers or local
state. A timeout, non-200 response or malformed state hides the sheet. It does not
read or transfer name, group, contact, deadline, task text, teacher, file
metadata, local draft content or device identifiers. The modal explicitly says
that Academic Salon is another site and a paid service.

Kladovaya suppresses the sheet when it detects any durable `kladovaya:*`
footprint, a service-worker-controlled returning session or the campaign's own
seen marker. A cleared/new browser or another device is inherently
indistinguishable from a new visitor; the Salon's server still denies the
discount when historical account/contact evidence appears.

The authenticated Salon owner receives a labelled, non-redeemable preview. The
Kladovaya bookmark `?offer_preview=welcome` is intentionally non-persistent and
preview-only: it removes the outbound activation CTA, writes no owner marker
and grants no entitlement. It is a safe visual preview URL rather than an
authenticated owner identity because the two sites have no shared login.

## Visual and interaction proof

Local real Chromium journeys covered Salon desktop light, mobile dark welcome,
mobile dark retention and forced colours, plus Kladovaya desktop/mobile,
owner-preview and forced colours. All tested dialogs were present, body scroll
was locked, the modal itself remained scrollable and horizontal overflow was
zero. Escape, backdrop/close, Tab/Shift+Tab containment, initial focus, inert
background and focus restoration were exercised. `pagehide` performs no
network request and hidden/inert system dialogs no longer block the modal slot.

The first forced-colours review found one real accessibility blocker: the Salon
primary CTA text was replaced by a white stripe under the dark theme. The final
rule now resolves the dark selector, uses explicit system colours,
`-webkit-text-fill-color` and `forced-color-adjust`. A fresh Chromium capture
showed the complete text `Подобрать первый этап со скидкой`, white on black,
at 463 x 50 px. The follow-up UX gate returned GO with P0=0/P1=0.

Architecture and economics reviews found and drove closure of the cross-site
kill-switch, guest-to-account, social-contact canonicalisation, cross-account
phone/username, Telegram welcome-bonus and exact Moscow-date boundary defects.
Architecture, economics and UX each returned final GO with P0=0/P1=0 on the
stable candidate. The follow-up economics check also confirmed the added
privacy copy for the HttpOnly intent cookie and Kladovaya seen marker.

The supplied bitmap art is original and isolated from the page until a visitor
passes the presentation gate. Suppressed Salon users therefore do not preload
the campaign image.

## Residual non-blocking limits

- The Salon waits roughly 4.2 seconds for another visible modal before
  deferring welcome until a later page entry; it never stacks over that modal.
- The Kladovaya preview URL is safe but not authenticated.
- A person who clears all first-party data or changes device can see a
  provisional sheet; the authoritative Salon order check remains fail-closed.
- The retention eligibility buckets are client assertions. A rotating-session
  attacker could consume the bounded 40-code daily issuance quota, but cannot
  increase the discount or bypass the atomic first-order claim.
- The current production installer checks the campaign tables, setting and
  seeded promo row after its transactional migration, but its read-only
  post-check does not independently attest every SQLite column/index/FK.
- Normalised historical-contact scans are intentionally exhaustive for this
  small database; they should move to an indexed contact-key registry if order
  volume grows materially.
- Production publication, live smoke and rollback/forward proof belong to the
  separate release workstream and are not asserted by this checkpoint.
