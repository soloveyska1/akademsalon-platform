## Why release163 was rejected

Release163 was assembled from release162 and exact canonical `9f9d5980`. Its
fresh 2,500 / 8,000 / 14,000 RUB routes and most saved-draft interactions
passed. After activation, however, real production Chromium exposed a path the
contract had not represented: with an actual saved support draft, selecting
support, opening the fictional specification and using Browser Back restored
the support radio and 14,000 RUB status but left the fixed dock as
`result=editing&route=page` / `Черновик`.

editing. It was classified P1, so release163 was immediately rolled back to
release162. External and VPS smoke passed 14/14 after rollback; the old page
