## Outcome

Release157 replaces the unreliable legacy visit dashboard with a server-owned
Analytics v2 contract. The Russian master dashboard shows the consented sample
for one consistent period: anonymous visitors and sessions, current activity,
time, entry/exit pages, safe sources and allowlisted campaigns, devices,
browsers, operating systems, approximate geography, page transitions, a strict
six-stage funnel, conversions, safe event chronology, JavaScript/form errors
and collection health. Server queries, not a browser-side slice, calculate every
metric and paginate the complete matching session set.

The public contract covers 87 measured pages, 45 allowlisted events and six
ordered funnel stages. `event_id`, `occurred_at` and `client_sequence` make
retries idempotent and preserve offline occurrence time and equal-millisecond
order. Thirty-minute sessionisation is atomic under concurrency. A signed,
network-bound six-hour grant, event/IP/visitor limits, edge body/rate limits and
strict validation reduce duplicate and poisoned traffic; data-health counters
make invalid, duplicate, throttled and server-failed input visible.

## Privacy and meaning boundary

- Collection starts only after separate analytics consent. A fresh real browser
  made zero requests to `/api/analytics/*` and `/api/visit` before consent.
- “Visitor” is an anonymous random browser identifier, not an identified person.
  It is intentionally not linked to a login, contact or order.
- Raw IP, raw user-agent, contact, user/order IDs, query text, form/text/file
  contents, OAuth data and raw referrers do not exist in the v2 raw schema.
  IP is used transiently for a local coarse lookup, signed-grant binding and
  abuse limits; only safe device categories and approximate geography remain.
- Approximate geography is resolved on the host from DB-IP City Lite, not sent
  to a remote lookup API. The dashboard visibly attributes DB-IP / CC BY 4.0
  and loads no DB-IP runtime or resource.
- Raw events have a maximum 365-day lifetime. A signed revoke deletes the
  matching visitor, sessions and events transactionally and leaves a bounded
  hashed tombstone only to reject offline replay.
- Coverage remains the consented first-party sample. Refusal, blockers and
  bots excluded by the default filter make “all people on the internet” an
  impossible and deliberately unclaimed metric.

Old legacy rows were not migrated: their contract and accuracy are not strong
enough to mix with v2. Therefore trustworthy history starts with release157;
the new dashboard can be empty until the first consented visit and must never
pretend that pre-release traffic belongs to this series.
